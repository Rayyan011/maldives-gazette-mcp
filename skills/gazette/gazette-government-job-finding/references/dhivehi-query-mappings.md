## Query method

For a phrase such as “admin officer,” search in this order:

1. `އެޑްމިނިސްޓްރޭޓިވް އޮފިސަރ`
2. `އެޑްމިނިސްޓްރޭޓިވް`
3. `އޮފިސަރ`
4. The original English phrase only as a fallback

Do not claim a Thaana term is an official translation unless it appears in the source. It is a search variant.
